ML Foundations demos

기계학습기초 · Hongik University · 2026-2. Each demo runs in the browser; nothing is installed.

Week 4, Hours 1 and 2

Linear regression by gradient descent, line by line

Steps through the thirteen-line gradient-descent loop one line, one point or one epoch at a time: each point's error, its contribution to the two derivatives, the update, and the loss. Includes a learning rate that is too large, so that the loss diverges, a switch that standardizes x, and a finite-difference check of the gradient.
Week 4, Hour 3

Logistic regression by gradient descent, line by line

The same loop with the prediction passed through the sigmoid: the probability curve, the decision boundary moving as it trains, and why this loss has no formula for its minimum.
Week 4, self-study

Naive Bayes spam filter, clue by clue

Multiply the prior by each word's probability for spam and for normal mail, compare the two scores, and see the same calculation as a sum of log-odds, the form logistic regression learns.
